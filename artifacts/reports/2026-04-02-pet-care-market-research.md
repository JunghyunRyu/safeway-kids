## 6. Market Gaps — What Korean Apps Are Missing

### 6.1 Real-Time GPS Tracking During Walks

**Current state in Korea:**
- 펫피, 산책가자: GPS for owner self-walks only (NOT for hired walkers)
- 도그메이트: Action cam recording, but NOT real-time GPS route tracking
- 와요: LIVE video streaming, but NOT GPS map-based route tracking
- 펫봄, 펫플래닛: Photo/video updates, no GPS

**Global standard:** 92% of international apps offer GPS tracking. Wag! provides real-time route visibility as a standard feature.

**GAP: No Korean app combines professional walker matching WITH real-time GPS route tracking on a map.** This is the single largest feature gap.

### 6.2 Integrated & Transparent Payment

**Current state in Korea:**
- Most apps handle payment through the platform (basic)
- No subscription/recurring billing options
- No transparent pricing comparison
- No tipping or bonus features

**Global standard:** Rover and Wag! have fully integrated payment with transparent fee structures, tipping, and automatic recurring billing.

**GAP: Korean apps lack subscription models, recurring booking automation, and transparent fee breakdowns.**

### 6.3 Scheduling & Recurring Bookings

**Current state in Korea:**
- All Korean apps are primarily one-time booking focused
- No calendar-based recurring walk scheduling
- No automated weekly/daily walk plans
- Family coordination (산책가자 does this for self-walks, but not for hired walkers)

**Global standard:** Rover supports recurring bookings. Tails emphasizes ongoing relationships.

**GAP: No Korean app offers automated recurring walk scheduling with the same matched walker.**

### 6.4 Walker Safety Verification & Quality

**Current state in Korea:**
- 도그메이트: 12:1 pass rate, interviews, home assessment — best vetting
- 와요: 10% acceptance, government docs, personality tests — strong
- 펫봄: Insurance coverage but lighter vetting
- Others: Minimal or no professional vetting

**Global problems:**
- Wag!: Uses independent contractors, limited training, 64,000+ reliability complaints (2023)
- Rover: Wide quality variance, owner does most vetting
- Industry-wide: Walker no-shows, inconsistent training

**GAP: Opportunity for deeper verification — dog handling skill tests, emergency training certification, real-time monitoring, and post-walk behavior reports. No Korean app does skill-based matching (like Tails in Chicago).**

### 6.5 AI-Powered Features

**Current state in Korea:**
- 핏펫/TTcare: AI for health diagnostics (not walking)
- 펫나우: AI for biometric ID (not walking)
- Walking apps: Zero AI integration

**Global trend:** 18% of users want AI-driven pet behavior tracking, predictive scheduling, and walker-dog compatibility scoring.

**GAP: No Korean app uses AI for walker-pet matching, walk optimization, behavior analysis during walks, or predictive scheduling.**

### 6.6 Comprehensive Platform (Super App)

**Current state:** Korean pet apps are fragmented:
- Walking tracking: 펫피, 산책가자
- Pet sitting: 도그메이트, 와요
- Shopping: 펫프렌즈
- Health: 핏펫, 펫닥
- Adoption: 포인핸드
- Travel: 반려생활

**GAP: No Korean super-app integrates walking, sitting, health, shopping, and community into one platform.** PetFriends (펫프렌즈) is closest but focuses on e-commerce.

## 7. Trends 2025-2026

### 7.1 Korean Market Trends

1. **Pet Humanization (펫휴머니제이션):** Pets treated as family members. 78% of owners report satisfaction. MZ generation driving premium spending.

2. **Cat Ownership Surge:** +9.2% YoY for cats vs -1.8% for dogs. Cat-specific services underserved.

3. **Veterinary Cost Crisis:** +78% in 2 years. Driving demand for preventive health tech and insurance.

4. **Pet Insurance Growth:** Only 12.8% penetration vs. 40%+ in UK/Sweden. MyBrown launched as Korea's first pet-only insurer (Aug 2025). Pawchi raised $4.5M for insurance.

5. **Regulation Changes:** Pets allowed in cafes/restaurants from March 2026. Dog meat ban full enforcement Feb 2027.

6. **Conglomerate Entry:** Shinhan ($67M invested), LG Uplus, Lotte, Shinsegae all entering pet market.

7. **Government Support:** $25M+ allocated for pet tech R&D.

### 7.2 Global Tech Trends

1. **GPS as Table Stakes:** 92% of apps now offer GPS. Route tracking, walk summaries, photo updates are expected.

2. **AI Integration:** Behavior tracking, predictive scheduling, walker-dog compatibility scoring.

3. **On-Demand Economy:** Sub-1-hour dispatch (Wag! model) gaining traction.

4. **Consolidation:** Blackstone's $2.3B Rover acquisition; Wag! bankruptcy. Market polarizing.

5. **Skill Verification:** Tails' model of in-person capability assessment (beyond background checks).

6. **Wearable Integration:** Smart collars (Halo Collar), GPS trackers, activity monitors integrating with walking apps.

### 7.3 User Pain Points (from review analysis)

**Korean users complain about:**
- Cannot see what happens during the walk (only get photos after)
- Limited geographic coverage outside Seoul
- Inconsistent sitter quality
- Negative reviews reportedly hidden on some platforms
- No way to verify actual walk route/distance
- Expensive for regular/daily use
- Hard to find same sitter repeatedly

**Global users complain about:**
- Walker no-shows (64,000+ complaints in 2023)
- Different walker every time (Wag!)
- High commission eating into walker pay (40% at Wag!)
- Poor customer service / can't reach humans
- Billing errors and disputed charges
- Walkers not trained for emergencies
- No real oversight during walks

## 9. Competitive Opportunity Summary

### What a new Korean pet walking app MUST have (table stakes):
1. Professional walker matching with verified profiles
2. In-app payment processing
3. Photo/video updates during care
4. Basic background check for walkers
5. Insurance/safety guarantee

### What would differentiate (competitive advantages):
1. **Real-time GPS route tracking** — THE biggest gap in Korean market
2. **Recurring booking automation** — weekly/daily walk schedules with same walker
3. **Skill-based walker matching** — beyond background checks, actual capability assessment
4. **AI-powered features** — behavior analysis, optimal walker matching, predictive scheduling
5. **Family coordination dashboard** — multiple family members managing pet care
6. **Integrated health tracking** — walk data feeding into health insights
7. **Cat care inclusion** — 9.2% growth segment, underserved
8. **Transparent pricing with subscription options** — monthly plans for regular walkers
9. **Walker quality scoring** — granular ratings (punctuality, care quality, route adherence, etc.)
10. **Emergency protocols** — in-app vet contact, nearest vet routing, incident reporting

### Addressable market estimate:
- 15.46M pet owners in Korea
- ~500B won ($370M) pet sitting market
- If 5% convert to a walking app at ₩100K/month avg spend:
  - ~770K users x ₩1.2M/year = ~₩924B TAM
- Realistic initial target: Seoul metro area (50% of pet households)
  - ~385K potential users in Seoul metro
